A mutually-exclusive event's outcome prices should sum to $1. Naively they seem to violate this often — but at the mid they sum to ~1, and the "violations" are the bid-ask spread plus a long tail of illiquid outcomes you cannot actually buy. This page tracks, live, whether a genuinely executable sub-$1 lock ever opens.

Executable lock cost over time

Median & minimum cost to buy one YES share in every outcome of a complete field (order size 100). Below the red $1 line would be real arbitrage.

Liquidity structure (latest snapshot)

Field completeness collapses with size: small dense fields are lockable; large fields list a long illiquid tail.
